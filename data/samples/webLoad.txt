Widespread use of USB drives and email as primary file transfer tools.


Rise of remote work and multi-device workflows highlighted the need for accessible file storage.


File versioning and lost data were common frustrations among professionals and students.


Existing tools like YouSendIt, FTP, and email attachments were unreliable, hard to use, or limited in size.


The cloud was a growing concept but not mainstream; trust in online storage was still limited.


Google Drive and iCloud did not exist yet.

Initial Customer Base:
College students moving between labs, dorms, and classrooms.


Developers and startup teams needing collaborative file access.


Freelancers and consultants with multiple devices.
