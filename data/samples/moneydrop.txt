Market Research:
Boom in app development and online software tools meant many developers needed to accept payments.


PayPal, Braintree, and Authorize.net were dominant but slow, clunky, and required merchant accounts.


Setting up a payment gateway took weeks and required bank involvement.


Hacker News forums were filled with complaints about payments integration.


Developers wanted clean APIs, testing environments, and fast onboarding.

Initial Customer Base:
Solo developers building side projects.


Startup teams launching SaaS apps.


Mobile developers looking to integrate payments without heavy SDKs.
