    What comes out.

    An excerpt from the computer-science education run. Full outputs and all three judge transcripts are published in the repository.

    Persona

    Alex Thompson, 20

    Undergraduate, computer science · San Francisco

    Goals
    Gain skills in AI and machine learning. Secure an internship. Graduate with a job offer.
    Pain points
    Outdated curriculum. Few industry connections. Little practical coursework.

    Hypotheses

    1. Adding AI, machine learning and data science to the curriculum will raise student satisfaction and job placement.
    2. Internships and guest lectures from industry will improve practical skills and employability.
    3. Training faculty in practical teaching will improve teaching effectiveness and outcomes.

    Built as software, not a notebook.

    Package and API

    An installable package with a FastAPI service for asynchronous research jobs, polling, and JSON artifacts saved for inspection.

    Agents, separated

    Web market research, persona generation and role-play interviews, and hypothesis synthesis are separate agents with their own prompts.

    Reproducible record

    Generated outputs, judge transcripts, known settings and their limits are documented, including what was not retained.
